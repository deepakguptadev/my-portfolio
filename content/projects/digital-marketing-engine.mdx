## Overview

A centralized digital marketing engine that lets marketing teams create, manage and launch campaigns across multiple social-media platforms. It brings campaign management, content scheduling, audience targeting, channel integration, performance analytics and user management into one interface.

## Context

The engine is made of domain-based microservices built on an API-first, cloud-native architecture. Microservices talk to the frontend applications and to each other through RESTful APIs, and the frontend is split into domain-oriented micro frontends.

## My role

Senior Software Engineer: designing UI wireframes and translating them into production code, and debugging and fixing issues during development.

## Engineering challenges

- **Scaling frontend domains.** Campaigns, scheduling, targeting, channels, analytics and users each formed their own frontend domain.
- **Managing service boundaries.** Each backend domain lived in its own microservice.
- **Cross-domain communication.** Micro frontends and microservices had to coordinate across domains.
- **API contract consistency.** Many frontends and services depended on the same RESTful contracts.
- **Distributed-service failures.** A failing service had to surface clearly in the UI.
- **Shared UI versus domain autonomy.** Consistent UI had to coexist with independently developed domains.

## Decisions & tradeoffs

| Decision        | Why                                  | Trade-off                         |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Micro Frontends | Independent domain and team delivery | Runtime and deployment complexity |
| Microservices   | Backend domain isolation             | Distributed complexity            |
| API-first       | Clear contracts                      | Governance required               |
| Redux           | Predictable state                    | Potential coupling                |
| Automated CI/CD | Repeatable delivery                  | Pipeline maintenance              |
